INSELMAN DIGITAL — ANALYTICS / CONSENT SETUP

Behavior in this build:
- Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity load automatically after a 3-second grace period unless the visitor chooses "Essential only".
- "Allow analytics" loads them immediately and remembers the choice.
- "Essential only" stores an opt-out and disables future analytics loading on later pages/visits in the same browser.
- The banner stays small and non-invasive.

IMPORTANT COMPLIANCE NOTE:
This is an OPT-OUT configuration, not prior opt-in consent. It is not appropriate by itself where prior consent is legally required (for example, many EEA/UK/Swiss visitors). If you want broader international compliance, use region-aware consent or opt-in-by-default for those visitors.

TRACKING IDS:
Each page expects these attributes on the <body> tag:
  data-ga-id="G-XXXXXXXXXX"
  data-clarity-id="YOUR_CLARITY_PROJECT_ID"

Example:
  <body data-ga-id="G-ABC1234567" data-clarity-id="abcd1234ef">

If the IDs are blank, no analytics provider can load even when analytics are allowed/defaulted.
