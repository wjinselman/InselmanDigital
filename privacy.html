Privacy

Updated October 2, 2026

Project inquiries

When you send a project request, we receive the name, company, email, phone number, project details, and timing information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond and discuss your project. Please do not include passwords, account credentials, or sensitive personal records.

The online form sends your request through FormSubmit to our business email. FormSubmit states that it keeps submission details in its archive for 30 days. An acceptance message means the submission service accepted the request; it does not promise immediate email delivery or a booked project.

Usage analytics

This website uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand visits and improve the site. Analytics stay off until you choose Allow analytics. Choosing Essential only keeps both services off. Your explicit choice is remembered in this browser, and Analytics preferences at the bottom of each page lets you change it. Withdrawing permission reloads the page to stop the analytics services. Contact options work regardless of your choice.

These services may use cookies or similar technologies and collect browsing and interaction information. Project-form content is masked in session recordings. Our custom inquiry and contact events do not include your name, email address, phone number, or message.

Other services

Links to client sites, email, phone, messaging, and other services are subject to those services’ own privacy practices.
