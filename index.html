After the launch

You’ll know what comes next.

What if I need changes six months later?

Send the changes you need. We’ll confirm the scope and price before work begins. A new page, a feature, or a larger redesign can be handled as a separate update; regular changes can be covered by an agreed support plan.

Are hosting and other subscriptions included?

Your proposal will list what is included and identify any separate hosting, domain, email, or third-party software costs. Tools such as booking and membership platforms may have their own recurring fees.

Who owns the site and controls the accounts?

Ownership, source-code handoff, account access, and third-party licenses are spelled out in your project agreement before development starts.

What happens if something stops working?

We’ll agree on post-launch support, how to report an issue, and what the support arrangement covers. New features and changes in scope are priced separately.
