Station readiness, not paperwork theater

One place to see whether the station is actually ready.

Station Handler™ is designed around a simple operational question: if the tones dropped right now, what would keep the department from responding effectively? The system brings apparatus, people, equipment and compliance signals into one readiness view.

01 · Apparatus

Ready means more than “it was checked.”

Track battery health, monitoring freshness and apparatus status so stale data never masquerades as a green check.

02 · Roster

Know who is response-ready.

Roster status separates compliant, due soon, action needed and setup-needed conditions without pretending untracked requirements are complete.

03 · Equipment

Gear and apparatus in one operational layer.

Keep equipment readiness close to the apparatus and people who depend on it instead of buried across disconnected lists.

04 · Training

Turn expiration dates into action.

Surface upcoming requirements and overdue items so training readiness is visible before it becomes a response or compliance problem.

05 · Gateway health

Monitor the monitor.

A truck cannot be confidently READY if the sensor or bay gateway has gone stale. Monitoring-path health is treated as first-class data.

06 · Demo Mode

Show the whole workflow safely.

Load a synthetic station scenario, demonstrate the readiness story, then restore the exact pre-demo state without contaminating operational data.

The hardware wedge

Know if the truck will start before somebody needs it.

The Battery Readiness module is designed around inexpensive BLE sensors today and a broader adapter model tomorrow. A stationary bay gateway receives local sensor data, forwards it over station Wi‑Fi, queues through outages, and tells Station Handler not only what the battery looked like—but whether that reading is still trustworthy.
