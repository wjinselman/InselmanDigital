How the limits work

You supply business photos, news, promotions, and approvals. Included edit and SEO time does not roll over. Additional revisions and work beyond these limits require an approved quote. New pages, redesigns, custom software, photography/video, paid advertising and ad spend, inbox management, and review responses are not included.

Content edits update existing text and images. New pages, new features, integrations, and design changes are separate projects. A small SEO improvement is a targeted change within the 30-minute allowance, not a new page or campaign.

Both plans cover one business and one website; Google support covers one profile. The managed plan covers one Facebook page and one Instagram account. Email/text support is for the included service during business hours, not unlimited consulting, meetings, or emergency response. Additional work is scoped and priced before it starts.

Compatibility, account access, ownership, payment schedule, minimum term if applicable, cancellation, and continuation are confirmed in the written agreement. SEO work does not guarantee rankings or leads. Existing clients remain governed by their current agreements.
