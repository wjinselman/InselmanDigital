Ardmore digital marketing questions

What is included in your online marketing support?

The $599/month starting plan includes defined website care, Google profile upkeep and two posts, four original social posts shared to Facebook and Instagram, capped SEO improvements, and a monthly summary. Website builds and initial onboarding are separate. The $299/month starting plan focuses on upkeep without social posting.

Can I just get a website or a web page for my business?

Yes. A standalone custom website starts at $1,500, with the final quote based on scope and no required monthly plan. We can discuss whether a focused page or a larger website fits your services. Explore Ardmore website design.

Does this include paid Google or Facebook ads?

No. Paid advertising management is quoted separately, and advertising spend is additional. The managed plan focuses on your website, local search foundations, business profile, and regular social posts.

Do you answer social messages or respond to reviews?

Inbox management, review responses, extra posts, and additional social platforms are outside the standard plan and available by separate quote.

Can you help if I only have a Facebook page?

Yes. We can build a website to give customers a central source of service information and contact options, while keeping Facebook as another way to reach your audience.

Will this guarantee customers or top Google rankings?

No. We commit to the agreed work and reporting. Search visibility and sales depend on factors such as competition, location, demand, your offer, and how inquiries are handled.
