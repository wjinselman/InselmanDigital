Built around the operation

The information the yard needs, without the spreadsheet gymnastics.

Cattle Handler connects fast field entry with the records and visibility needed later in the office.

01 · Treatment

Fast field entry

Record cattle number, pen, medicine, dosage, dart use, notes, and treatment details from a phone without slowing the work down.

02 · Pens

Live pen management

Keep pens organized with owner assignments, head counts, treated cattle, activity, and the operational picture in one place.

03 · History

90-day cattle history

Search cattle records and see treatment history, dates, medication usage, pen placement, and the active animal window accurately over time.

04 · Medicine

Medication tracking

Track medicine, cc amounts, darts, pricing, and treatment totals so the record is useful for both cattle care and billing.

05 · Shared data

Cloud-connected users

Authorized users work from the same operational data rather than separate text threads, handwritten notes, or isolated phone records.

06 · Reporting

Office-ready records

Turn the work already being entered in the yard into searchable reporting, owner history, exports, and operational summaries.
