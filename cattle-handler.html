Before you get started

A few good
questions.

Is this a treatment recommendation app?

Cattle Handler focuses on recording the treatments your team chooses, handling medicine inventory, and organizing reports. Your treatment decisions stay with your operation and veterinarian.

Does “pay as you use” mean per treatment?

No. It’s $99 for each active subscription month, per operation. Cancel renewal when you no longer need it and restart for another paid month when your season begins. The $750 setup fee is paid once.

What happens to our records in the off-season?

We agree on your record-retention settings during setup and explain what remains available while inactive. Canceling your subscription does not promise permanent record storage. Export the reports you need before records reach your operation’s deletion date.

Can the crew use their phones?

Yes. Cattle Handler is designed for phone and desktop browsers. We’ll walk through your devices and connectivity needs during your demo.

Can you set it up for our operation?

Yes. We handle your pens, users, and medicine setup, then walk your crew through the everyday workflow. Requests for new custom features are scoped and priced separately.
